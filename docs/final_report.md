# Final project report: Tropicast v0.2

> Generated by `scripts/make_final_report.py` from `reports/*.json`. Metrics are copied, never typed. Experimental research output; not an official forecast.

## 1. Summary
Tropicast is an open research platform for North Indian Ocean tropical cyclones. It ingests IBTrACS (RSMC New Delhi), SHIPS IO environmental predictors and HURSAT-B1 IR imagery. It trains leakage-safe track, intensity, rapid-intensification, satellite-intensity and centre-localisation models; calibrates their uncertainty; verifies them on unseen seasons; and serves everything through a FastAPI service and dashboard with full provenance.
